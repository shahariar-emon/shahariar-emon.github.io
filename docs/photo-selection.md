# Photo selection

Reviewed all 79 images visually using contact sheets; inspected the 15 shortlisted photographs at larger size. Selected 15; excluded 64.

| Category | Kept / reviewed | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Music | 1 / 3 | One clear Physics Day performance; omit the near duplicate and wider ensemble view. |
| Photography | 8 / 29 | Balance trees, reflection, macro details, architecture, bridge geometry, flowers and clouds; avoid identifiable street subjects, screenshots, repetitive tree/sky studies and noisy or dark night images. |
| KARL | 1 / 6 | Research presentation provides work context; omit social meals, group portraits, news screenshot and composite. |
| MBL | 1 / 7 | Trajectory analysis directly represents computational work; omit first-salary handover, ceremonial groups and extra staff/training scenes. |
| Sakura Science | 2 / 15 | Presentation and completion certificate tell the exchange story; omit sightseeing, large groups and repetitive visits/discussions. |
| Conferences | 2 / 19 | One poster discussion and one Best Paper Award handover; omit near duplicates, broad group pictures, distant/poorly lit presentations and extra ceremonies. |

## Captions and privacy

Captions derive from archive category, descriptive filenames and visible content. No identities of other participants are inferred. Dates appear only when stated in the selected filename. Existing CV-derived site text is preserved. Other people remain only in relevant academic/performance contexts; consent cannot be established from the supplied files. No salary photo or unrelated social imagery is included. No new dates, institutions or awards were inferred from appearances.

Original proportions and colour are retained in optimized WebP files. Site presentation uses CSS grayscale; linked files show colour. EXIF, GPS, IPTC and XMP metadata are absent. Two responsive sizes per photo, up to 480 and 1280 pixels on the longest edge; lazy loading and explicit dimensions. Combined new assets: 2,150,888 bytes (about 2.1 MB). Originals and rejected photos are not committed.
